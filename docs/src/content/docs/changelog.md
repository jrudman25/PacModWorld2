## 0.1.0 — in development

Not yet released. Targets Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

- **B-Doing**: 3x3 bounce pad that launches players and mobs about 6 blocks
  into the air, with no fall damage on landing
- **Red Rat**: small hostile mob spawning in grassy biomes, with flame breath
  and fire charge drops
- PacModWorld2 creative tab containing all mod items, including a Red Rat
  spawn egg
