---
title: B-Doing
description: A 3x3 bounce pad block from Pac-Man World 2.
---

The B-Doing is a 3x3 bounce pad. Landing on it launches players and mobs about
6 blocks into the air, and landing on it never causes fall damage. Sneak to
stand on it without bouncing.

## Crafting

Slime blocks in the corners, iron ingots on the edges, and a piston in the
center.
