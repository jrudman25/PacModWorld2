---
title: B-Doing
description: A 3x3 bounce pad block from Pac-Man World 2.
---

The B-Doing is a 3x3 bounce pad. Landing on it launches players and mobs about
6 blocks into the air, and landing on it never causes fall damage.

## Bouncing

- Jump or fall onto the pad to bounce. Walking onto it does not launch you.
- Sneak to stand on it without bouncing.
- Mobs bounce the same way players do.

## Placing and breaking

- The B-Doing covers a 3x3 area centered on the block you place it on. All
  nine spaces must be free, or it will not place.
- Breaking any part removes the whole pad and drops one B-Doing (no drop in
  Creative mode).
- Pistons cannot push it.

## Crafting

Slime blocks in the corners, iron ingots on the edges, and a piston in the
center.
