The Red Rat is a small hostile mob that hops around grassy biomes at any time
of day.

## Spawning

Red Rats spawn in groups of 1 to 3 on grass blocks in these biomes:

- Plains and Sunflower Plains
- Meadow
- Forest, Flower Forest, and Birch Forest
- Savanna

Unlike most hostile mobs, they do not need darkness to spawn. They do not
spawn on Peaceful difficulty.

## Behavior

A Red Rat targets nearby players and hops toward them. Once within about 3.5
blocks, it stops and breathes a short cone of flame roughly every 2.5 seconds.

| Stat | Value |
| :-- | :-- |
| Health | 8 (4 hearts) |
| Flame breath damage | 2 (1 heart), plus 3 seconds of fire |
| Flame breath reach | About 4 blocks |

The flame breath can hit any creature caught in the cone, but never other Red
Rats, and it never burns blocks. Red Rats are immune to fire.

## Drops

0 to 1 fire charge. Looting increases the maximum by 1 per level.

## Creative mode

A Red Rat spawn egg is available in the PacModWorld2 creative tab, alongside
all of the mod's other items.
