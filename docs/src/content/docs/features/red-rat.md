The Red Rat is a small hostile mob that hops around grassy biomes — plains,
meadows, forests, and savannas — at any time of day.

## Behavior

It attacks with a short-range flame breath that sets targets on fire but never
burns blocks. It is immune to fire.

## Drops

Fire charges.

## Creative mode

A Red Rat spawn egg is available in the PacModWorld2 creative tab, alongside
all of the mod's other items.
